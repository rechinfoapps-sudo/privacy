Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Overview

MAGIS TV LLC ("Magis TV", "we", "our", or "us"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and services.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal data as possible. We do not require you to provide sensitive personal identification to browse content. When using our app, the following non-personal data may be collected automatically:

• Device information (such as your device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers).
• Diagnostic data, crash logs, and app performance metrics to keep the app fast and reliable.
• Advertising ID for serving relevant ads and analytics.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to operate and improve the application:

• Displaying up-to-date movie & series details, trailers, and streaming guides.
• Fixing crashes and technical bugs.
• Understanding which features are most useful to our users.
• We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services & Partners

To provide movie metadata and support free access via advertising, our app integrates with trusted third-party service providers. Each provider operates under its own privacy policy:

Advertising & Tracking

We partner with third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, and Meta) to serve advertisements. These partners may use device identifiers and cookies to serve ads based on your general interests, in compliance with applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA).

Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Your Choices & Rights

You can manage or reset your advertising identifier directly from your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You may also request deletion of any diagnostic data associated with your device by reaching out to us.
