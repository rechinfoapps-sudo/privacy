Privacy Policy

Applicable to: Stopwatch - Easy Lap Timer | Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Our application operates entirely offline on your local device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal user data. Your privacy is 100% protected.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AHMED DEVSELL LLC ("we", "our", or "us") handles user privacy for the Stopwatch - Easy Lap Timer mobile application. We are committed to transparency and compliance with Google Play Developer policies and global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

2. Data Collection & Usage

Stopwatch - Easy Lap Timer is designed as a standalone utility tool. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, location, or device identifiers.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: The application does not use analytics SDKs, trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • Local Data Storage: All stopwatch timing results, lap records, and user preferences (such as display settings or vibration toggles) are stored locally in the temporary memory of your device and are never transmitted to any external server.

3. Device Permissions

The application only accesses necessary hardware features directly related to core stopwatch functionality:

  • Vibration (Haptic Feedback): Used solely to provide brief physical feedback when buttons (Start, Stop, Lap) are pressed.
  • Wake Lock: Used solely to prevent the screen from sleeping while the stopwatch or countdown timer is actively running, if enabled by the user.

The app does not request access to contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or precise location.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This application contains zero third-party advertisements and integrates no third-party data tracking SDKs. We do not share any data with marketing companies or data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

6. Security

Because no data leaves your mobile device, your data is as secure as your personal device. We recommend keeping your Android OS updated to ensure maximum device security.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
